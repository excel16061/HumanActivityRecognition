Fast processed 121 clips in 23.054 s (median inference 94.549 ms). Balanced processed 301 clips in 42.132 s (median inference 97.569 ms). Fast is approximately 1.83× faster in total runtime; Balanced samples approximately 2.49× as many clips. No ground-truth accuracy comparison is available.

03 / EXPERIMENTAL METHOD

Transparent by design.

01 / BASELINE

Consistent preprocessing

16-frame clips, 112 × 112 spatial input, model-specific mean subtraction and RGB channel ordering. Keep preprocessing identical across runtime comparisons.

02 / SAMPLING

Configurable coverage

Fast samples every 5 seconds; Balanced samples every 2 seconds. Both produce timestamped predictions and latency reports.

03 / REPRODUCIBILITY

Evidence over claims

Record the device, software environment, video duration, clip count, runtime, and median inference. Accuracy requires independently verified labels.

05 / RESEARCH INTEGRITY

What remains open.

!

Recognition accuracy not yet validated

Predicted activity names are not ground truth. The current demonstration videos lack a completed independent annotation set, so no Top-1 accuracy, macro-F1, or class-level recall is claimed.

↗

Speed is workload-dependent

Measured times depend on sampling interval, video codec, decoding, CPU load, and device. Results from one video are not universal performance guarantees.

◇

Future directions

Evaluate on an independently labeled dataset, compare ONNX Runtime and Core ML execution, investigate adaptive sampling, and report accuracy–latency–memory trade-offs.
